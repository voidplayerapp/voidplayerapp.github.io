VOID Connect

Your own library

VOID Connect turns your sources into your own library with posters, without any external server. Everything runs right on your TV or phone. Write a source yourself, let an AI assistant draft it and test it right away through the VOID Connect API, or add a finished one with a single link.

VDownloader

Download, unpack, watch

Just give VOID links, including links to RAR archives. VOID downloads the files, unpacks archives automatically, split ones included, and saves everything straight to your TV, a USB drive or a NAS. You can start watching while it is still unpacking. If you like, downloads wait for the night.

Telegram

Your Telegram videos

Sign in with your Telegram account and play videos from your own chats and channels on your TV or phone, right next to the rest of your library.

IPTV

Live TV from your provider

Works with common IPTV providers: enter Xtream login details or an M3U link, on your TV or comfortably in the browser on your phone, and you’re set. Every channel sits in one timeline with its guide, and because VOID keeps the guide in a local database, it’s there instantly. Zap through your channels fast, catch up on what you missed if your provider keeps an archive, and record shows straight from the guide.

Stremio add-ons

Your add-ons, right inside VOID

Add Stremio-compatible add-ons with their link, on your TV or from your phone or PC. Their catalogs live in the add-on area, and when you press play, their sources appear in the same picker as your playlists. Paired devices pick up your add-ons too.

Privacy

Your data stays at home

VOID Player has no accounts and no tracking. Logins for playlists, file-hosting accounts and add-ons are stored on your devices and only ever go to the service they belong to, never to us. Your devices sync directly with each other, encrypted, with no cloud in between. From the internet, VOID only fetches artwork, descriptions, sports dates and updates. Your logins and your watch history never leave your devices.

Inside VOID Player

Smooth, even on low-end devices

Built for speed and smooth, even on budget TV boxes with 1.5 GB of RAM. On TVs it plays with Media3, Android’s own player, through your device’s hardware decoder: 4K with HDR10 and Dolby Vision, Dolby and DTS audio straight to your receiver. On phones, mpv plays almost any format.

Live TV and guide

All your channels in one timeline. Start a show from the guide or catch up on what you missed, where your provider supports it.

Movies and series

VOID fills your library with posters, descriptions and cast from high-quality film databases. Continue where you stopped, on any of your devices.

Set up in your browser

Enter playlists (Xtream, M3U, SMB) and add-on links comfortably on your phone or PC. Everything stays local on your Wi-Fi.

Sync without the cloud

Pair your phone and TV once. History, favorites and settings stay the same on all your devices.

Recordings

Record from the guide on your TV. Watch later, or copy the recording to your phone.

Profiles

A profile for everyone at home, including a kids profile with its own rules.

Sports

Fixtures, results and live scores for your teams at a glance. See when and on which of your channels a match is on, and start it with one click.

Updates

New versions arrive inside the app. One confirmation and you are up to date.

Install

TV · Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV

  1. Install Downloader from your TV's app store.
  2. Open it and enter the code 8238632, or voidplayerapp.github.io/tv.
  3. Allow the installation when asked, then open VOID Player and enter your access code.

Phone

  1. Open voidplayerapp.github.io/phone in your phone's browser.
  2. Open the downloaded file and allow the installation.
  3. Open VOID Player and enter your access code.
